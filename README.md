# CustomGento_AiImageHint

Label AI generated and AI edited product images in your Magento 2 store. Mark an image as *AI generated* or
*AI modified* in the admin, and the storefront shows the official EU icon for labelling AI content on it,
everywhere that image appears.

## Why you need it

More and more shops use AI to create product images or to retouch real photos: new backgrounds, removed
reflections, lifestyle scenes that were never shot. Customers have a right to know when what they see is not
a real photograph of the product.

- **EU AI Act.** The AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) brings transparency obligations for AI generated and
  AI manipulated content. They apply from 2 August 2026.
- **Consumer protection.** A product image that looks like a photo but was generated or altered by AI can
  mislead customers about what they are buying.
- **Trust.** A clear, consistent label shows customers you are open about how your images are made.

Magento has no way to flag an image as AI content. This module adds it, per image, and takes care of showing
the label on the storefront. Whether and how your images must be labelled depends on your case; this module
gives you the tool, not legal advice.

## Features

- **Per image labelling in the admin.** Every product image can be marked as *No AI used*, *AI generated* or
  *AI modified*, right in the image's detail view.
- **Readable on every photo.** Pick a black or a white icon per image, whichever stands out better against it.
- **Shown everywhere the image appears.** One setting in the admin, and the label follows the image across the
  storefront, see *Where the label shows*.
- **The official EU icons.** A labelled icon ("AI generated" / "AI modified") on the large product image, a
  compact AI mark on thumbnails and small images.
- **Accessible.** Each icon carries a plain explanation as alternative text and tooltip, so customers can hover
  for it and screen readers announce it:
  - *AI generated*: "This image was fully generated by artificial intelligence (AI) and is not a real
    photograph of the product."
  - *AI modified*: "This image is based on a real product photograph and was edited using artificial
    intelligence (AI)."
- **Translatable.** All texts go through Magento's translations; German is included.
- **Labelled per product.** When several products share the same image file, each product keeps its own
  labelling.
- **Kept on duplicate.** A duplicated product keeps the labels of its images.
- **Safe with imports and integrations.** Labels change only when someone changes them in the admin. Product
  imports, the API and other integrations that save products leave them untouched.
- **Works with Magento's own admin gallery.** The fields are added to the core image detail view, which keeps all
  of its own settings, store view options included.
- **Nothing to configure.** Install it, label your images, done.

## Where the label shows

| Place | Label |
| --- | --- |
| Product page gallery, main image (also in fullscreen) | labelled icon: "AI generated" / "AI modified" |
| Product page gallery thumbnails | small AI mark |
| Category pages and search results | small AI mark |
| Product widgets, related products, up-sells, cross-sells | small AI mark |
| Shopping cart page | small AI mark |
| Minicart | small AI mark |
| Checkout order summary | small AI mark |
| Wishlist and product comparison pages | small AI mark |

The label sits in the bottom right corner of the image, so it does not collide with the Sale / New labels themes
usually place top left.

On category pages, when a customer picks a colour swatch and the tile shows another image, the label of the
original image is hidden until that image is shown again.

## Not covered yet

- **Images of configurable product options.** When a customer picks an option and the gallery or listing tile
  shows that option's own images, those images show no label.
- **Recently viewed products, the wishlist and comparison sidebars, and search autocomplete.** These show no
  label.
- **Galleries that replace Magento's product page gallery.** Third party gallery or zoom extensions that render
  their own product page gallery instead of Magento's show no label there. The other places above are not
  affected.
- **Minicart after a change.** The minicart is cached in the customer's browser, so a label changed in the admin
  appears there after the next cart update.

## Requirements

- Magento 2.4 with the Luma theme or a theme based on it
- PHP 8.2 - 8.5

## Installation

```bash
composer require customgento/module-ai-image-hint-m2
bin/magento module:enable CustomGento_AiImageHint
bin/magento setup:upgrade
```

## Usage

1. Open *Catalog > Products*, edit a product and go to *Images And Videos*.
2. Click an image to open its *Image Detail* view.
3. Set **AI labelling** to *No AI used*, *AI generated* or *AI modified*.
4. If labelled, pick the **AI hint colour**: black or white, whichever reads better on that image.
5. Close the view and save the product.

The labelling belongs to the image itself, so it applies to all store views.

## Customising the look

The icons are styled with plain CSS classes, so your theme can move or resize them:

| Class | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| `.ai-image-hint` | every icon |
| `.ai-image-hint--full` | the labelled icon on the product page's main image |
| `.ai-image-hint--thumb` | the AI mark on product page thumbnails |
